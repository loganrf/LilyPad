Dash display

Any screen can show the live dash at /dash (no password, like the home screen). To also push reminders to an external dashboard (Home Assistant, Node-RED, TRMNL...), configure a webhook. See docs/DASH.md.

Dash clocks

Show a short message on Dash LED matrix clocks whenever a reminder or alert fires, directly, without Home Assistant in between. Follows each reminder's Dash switch.

Each alert queues its own message on the clock (up to 8), and acknowledging in LilyPad doesn't clear it: press the clock's middle button.
