Privacy & security

LilyPad is built for tired parents: logging needs no password, so anyone who can open this page can use it. That convenience has consequences you should understand. The full write-up is in docs/PRIVACY_AND_SECURITY.md in the project.

Open to anyone who can reach LilyPad

  • The home screen, the live dash (/dash) and its JSON feed (/api/dash).
  • The timeline of the last {{ window_hours }} hours, including notes.
  • Adding, editing and deleting entries from that window (deletes are reversible by an admin for 30 days).
  • Snoozing reminders and acknowledging alerts.

Requires the admin password

  • Anything older than {{ window_hours }} hours: full history, search and editing.
  • Reports (including the pediatrician summary) and all exports and backups.
  • Settings: baby details, time zone, notification and dash configuration, reminder schedules, vital parameters and their limits.
  • The trash and the notification delivery log.

What that means in practice

  • Keep LilyPad on your home network or a private VPN (WireGuard, Tailscale...). Do not port-forward it to the internet.
  • Anyone on your Wi-Fi (guests, a compromised smart device) can see and change recent entries.
  • Data is stored unencrypted in one SQLite file in the Docker volume. Protect and back up that volume.
  • ntfy notifications travel through the ntfy server you choose. On the public ntfy.sh, anyone who guesses your topic can read them. Use a long random topic, an access token, or self-host ntfy, and pick a notification detail level in settings.
  • LilyPad never sends data anywhere except the ntfy server and dash webhook you configure. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
  • Installing LilyPad as an app stores only its styles, scripts, logo and a "can't reach LilyPad" notice on the device. Entries are never stored there.

LilyPad is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. Example limits are not medical advice; confirm thresholds with your pediatrician.
